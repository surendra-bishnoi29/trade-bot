6 · The risk officer and the rules in force

Checked on every order · deterministic, no LLM
  • kill switch off (exits always pass)
  • symbol in the 30-name universe
  • whole shares, at least 1
  • a price is known
  • new price younger than 15 minutes while the market is open; unknown age rejects; exits exempt
  • order at most 5% of equity
  • position after the order at most 10% of equity
  • no short selling
  • market hours, or queue for the open
  • at most 20 orders a day (exits exempt)
Checked in the merge · before an order exists
  • a blocking objection from the skeptic turns a buy into nothing and a sell into a hold
  • conviction at least 0.40
  • new with a skeptic concern, conviction at least 0.50
  • stop between 2% and 8%, target at least 1.5 times the stop
  • at least two evidence ids that exist in the journal
  • 2% of equity per position, at most 10 open, at most 3 new per cycle
  • longs only, paper only

7 · Health, and what broke

Broker · Alpaca paper · OK, reconciled every 5 min, 1 failed call at 03:04 Price feed · IEX websocket, free · thin, died 19:58 Event bus · Redis unreachable, in-memory fallback Kill switch · off since 18:17 Scheduler · 2 cycles missed Desk process · not running at 03:30 Daily bars · 3 Nov bar is a mid-session snapshot
Defect 1 · the scheduler sleeps in one pieceIt computes the next cycle time and sleeps the whole way. A laptop sleep or a long stall then skips the cycle silently. Fix: sleep in 60-second slices, re-read the clock each time, run a missed cycle as soon as the desk is awake and the market is open, and log every miss.
Defect 2 · the desk ran on a laptop that sleptNo code survives that. Fix: start it with caffeinate -i uv run desk run, or run it on an always-on machine, and add a heartbeat line every five minutes so a gap is visible in the log.
Defect 3 · a half-day daily bar is kept foreverThe 3 Nov daily bar was fetched during the session (CVX 225.70 against a real close of 224.24, LLY 1433.19 against 1412.55) and the bar table never overwrites. The scorecard's 5-day and 20-day returns use it. Fix: overwrite on conflict and skip today's daily bar until the close.
Defect 4 · orders on the previous closePre-market and after-close cycles can queue market orders that fill at the open on yesterday's price; the AAPL test order did exactly that. Fix: the pre-market cycle plans only, and buys go out 15 minutes after the open on fresh bars. A design change, so it waits for your yes.
Observation · the free feed is thinQuiet names went 20 to 30 minutes without a bar during the session (PLD and UNP last printed at 19:30, JPM, GS, XOM, LIN and BRK.B at 19:32 before the outage). With the 15-minute freshness rule, buys in such names will sometimes be rejected. The paid SIP feed is the trigger we defined for that.
