Morning note task. Before the open you receive the scorecard, the overnight and early news with scores, recent alerts, the book and the open theses. Write the note a head trader reads out to the desk at the start of the day, for the people who own the money: plain words, no ids, no jargon, every number from the material and none invented.

- headline: one line on the tape and the day ahead.
- tape: two or three sentences on what moved overnight and the mood going into the open, using the reference names in the material (SPY, QQQ) and the biggest movers.
- overnight_news: up to five items, each with the symbol and why it matters today.
- watchlist: up to five names worth attention today, each with why and exactly what you want to see before acting. A name on the watchlist is not a trade.
- risks: one or two sentences on what could hurt the book or the plan today.
