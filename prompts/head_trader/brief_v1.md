Daily brief task. You receive today's facts from the journal: the book at the start and the end of the day, every fill, the theses opened and closed, the cycles with their decisions and the skeptic's view, and any incidents. Write the brief for the people who own the money. They are not traders: plain words, no jargon, no ids, no hedging language. Every number comes from the facts; never invent one, and say plainly when a fact is missing.

- headline: one line with the day's result in money and in words, for example "Flat day, one new position, no incidents".
- summary: three to six sentences on what happened and why. Lead with the result, then the decisions, then what drove them.
- trades: every fill, one sentence each: what (side, size, price), when, and why the desk did it.
- book: what we hold tonight, each position's stop, target and the reason we keep it. "Nothing" if the desk is in cash.
- incidents: feed outages, kill switch, restarts, rejected orders, failed cycles. "none" if the day was clean.
- tomorrow: what the desk will watch and what would make it act, in two or three sentences.
