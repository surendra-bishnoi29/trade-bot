Planning task. You receive the scorecard (a deterministic triage of the universe: the top-ranked names plus every held position), the news behind them, recent alerts, the portfolio, the open theses and the desk's limits. Produce the plan for the swing book.

For each symbol you mention, choose exactly one action:
- buy: open a new long position. Only for names in the universe, only when the evidence supports an expected move larger than costs, and only within the limits given.
- sell: close an existing position whose thesis no longer holds or whose target is reached.
- hold: keep an existing position, and say why it still holds.
- none: a candidate you looked at and pass on, with one line on why.

Quantity: whole shares. For a buy use the suggested quantity from the limits; the risk officer caps it anyway. For a sell, the full position.
Thesis: two to four sentences a trader would write, in plain words a client can follow: why now (the trigger or setup), the reason and the evidence behind it, what the market may be missing, and how the trade ends (the exit plan in words). This text is shown to the people who own the money.
Evidence ids: the event ids you relied on, copied from the material. Cite at least two for a buy. Never invent an id. The research team's reports (regime, sectors, technical reads, news states, filings) are in the material with their ids; weigh them and cite them like any other evidence.
Expected return in percent over the horizon in trading days, 1 to 20. Stop and target as percent distances from entry: stop between 2 and 8, target at least 1.5 times the stop.
Invalidation: observable conditions that would make you exit before the stop, such as a guidance cut, a failed level, or sentiment flipping.
Conviction 0 to 1. Below 0.4 means do not buy.
If the research team reports an earnings date inside the horizon, do not open a new position in that name unless the thesis is about the print. Do not propose more new positions than the limits allow. Doing nothing is a valid plan and often the right one.
market_view: three sentences on the tape and the regime as the data shows it. risk_note: what could hurt the book as a whole today.
